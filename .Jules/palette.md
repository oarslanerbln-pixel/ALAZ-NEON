## 2024-05-18 - Missing ARIA labels in Admin Settings
**Learning:** Found that custom icon-only remove buttons (×) and visual toggle switches in `VenueSettings.tsx` were lacking `aria-label`s, rendering them inaccessible to screen readers. We need to ensure that even admin/internal tools have proper accessibility tags, not just the player-facing screens. The translations for screen reader text ("Kaldır", "Ödül Sistemini Kapat") should be explicitly added based on context.
**Action:** Always check custom toggles and "x" clear/remove buttons in admin and settings screens for missing `aria-label` attributes.
